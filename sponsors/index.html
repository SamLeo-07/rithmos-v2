LEADERSHIP / VISION & PEDIGREE

CRAFTING HIGH-VOLTAGE ENTERTAINMENT WITH OVER A DECADE OF CURATION EXCELLENCE.

Ravikanth Popuri is an accomplished entrepreneur, experiential curator, and entertainment architect known for conceiving and executing premier lifestyle, luxury, and cultural properties across South India.

Through Bottled Poetry and Madiza, Ravikanth pioneered high-profile activations introducing international wine brands (including Italian icon Pitars) to the Indian market. His expansive portfolio features royal palace fashion showcases with celebrated couture designers, luxury wine festivals, and red-carpet galas graced by leading film industry stalwarts, royal dignitaries, and corporate leaders.

With Right Hand Entertainments, Ravikanth channels this seasoned production mastery, deep network of artists, and institutional stakeholder relationships into RITHMOS — creating a transparent, merit-driven tournament circuit built specifically for authentic live musicians to rise to grand arena acclaim.
