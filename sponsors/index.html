COMMERCIAL INTEGRITY

GUARANTEED vs MEASURED vs PROJECTED

This distinction keeps the RITHMOS sponsorship proposition credible: we sell defined inventory, activate it professionally, and report the actual performance.

GUARANTEED

What RITHMOS contractually commits to deliver — placements, assets, activations and agreed partner benefits.

MEASURED

Actual performance tracked during and after the event — reach, impressions, views, engagement, attendance, leads and activation results.

PROJECTED

Campaign targets or estimates, clearly identified as projections and not guaranteed outcomes.
