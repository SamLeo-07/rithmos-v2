03 // TOURNAMENT BLUEPRINT

COMPETITION GUIDELINES & SELECTION CRITERIA

Authentic musicianship, live instrument execution, and original Telugu & regional connection.

DOWNLOAD TOURNAMENT RULEBOOK (PDF) ↓

LIVE INSTRUMENT STANDARDS

• Band must perform with live instruments (drums, guitars, bass, keys, brass, strings, Indian percussion).
• Pre-recorded backing tracks, backing vocals, or click-stem automation are strictly prohibited.
• Band performing language: Telugu mandatory (multilingual original compositions welcome).

TRANSPARENT SELECTION PROCESS

• 100% Free Entry: No registration fees, audition charges, or pass costs.
• Selection by the RITHMOS Event Management Panel based on live performance musicianship.
• All original songwriting rights remain 100% with the band members.
