ORIGIN // THE RITHMOS STORY

EVERY BAND HAS A STORY.
EVERY STORY NEEDS A STAGE. RITHMOS IS THE STAGE.

RITHMOS is a stage created for live bands, their music and the stories they have to tell. It is a celebration of talent, performance and the energy of live music — bringing bands and audiences together for moments that deserve to be heard, seen and remembered.

01 // THE ROOTS & AMBITION

THE RITHMOS STORY:
BORN IN HYDERABAD. REACHING THE WORLD.

Born in Hyderabad, the first edition - RITHMOS has a strong connection to the Telugu music-loving audience — in the city, across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, and among Telugu audiences around the world. Our ambition is to create a live-music property that begins here, speaks to this audience, and grows far beyond.

FOUNDATION 01

OUR PURPOSE

To create a credible, exciting and professionally produced platform where live bands can perform, compete, connect with audiences and open doors to new opportunities.

FOUNDATION 02

OUR MISSION

To discover, showcase and celebrate live-band talent by creating high-quality stages, meaningful audience experiences and opportunities that extend beyond a single performance.

FOUNDATION 03

OUR VISION

To build RITHMOS into a nationally recognised live-music property — a platform where bands aspire to perform, audiences look forward to experiencing, and partners see meaningful opportunities to connect with the music community.

04 // THE COMPANY BEHIND RITHMOS
THE COMPANY BEHIND RITHMOS

RIGHT HAND ENTERTAINMENTS

About Right Hand Entertainments

Right Hand Entertainments is a new entertainment and event management company built around an experienced team with a strong track record in creating and managing events and brand-led experiences.

The team behind Right Hand Entertainments previously created and operated Bottled Poetry, delivering a wide range of experiences across fashion, art, lifestyle and luxury, in association with the promotion and marketing of an international wine brand in India.

That experience — in event conceptualisation, production, venue management, brand integration, audience engagement and partner management — now comes together under Right Hand Entertainments, with a renewed focus on creating original entertainment properties.

RITHMOS is the first expression of that ambition.
