01 // THE ROOTS & AMBITION

THE RITHMOS STORY:

03 // THE BAND CREED

THE FOUR NON-NEGOTIABLE
STAGE COMMANDMENTS.

When you step onto the RITHMOS stage, you enter a pact with the crowd and the sound engineers.

01

PLAY WHAT YOU WRITE

Original songwriting is the lifeblood of rock music. Covers are respected, but championship points are heavily awarded to original riffs, distinctive arrangements, and authentic storytelling.

02

SWEAT ON THE WOODEN FLOOR

Rock is a physical, tactile sport. If your knuckles aren't bleeding into the guitar strings and your drummer isn't splintering sticks, you haven't given the room everything you have.

03

RESPECT THE SOUND CREW

The Front-of-House engineer is your sixth band member. Dial your amp stages in harmony during your 15-minute soundcheck, trust the monitor mix, and let the engineers blast your sound to the highest stadium row.

04

BACK YOUR FELLOW BANDS

RITHMOS is a united rock brotherhood. Applaud your competition, share patch cables in emergencies, and remember that when one independent live band rises, the entire live ecosystem rises with them.
